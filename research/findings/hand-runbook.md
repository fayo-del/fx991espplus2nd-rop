# Hand-only runbook — playable game on the physical fx-991ES PLUS 2nd ed (CY-845)

**Nothing extra needed**: no autotyper MCU, no PCB, no wires — just the calculator
and ~10–15 min of patient typing. Everything below is HW-verified or emulator-proven
(see `real03-complete.md`, `hardware-keypresses.md`, `fx991espplus2nd-rop/docs/hardware-procedure.md`).

## What you get
A self-running game loop on the LCD: a cell moves by itself each pass (drift), and the
**held arrow steers it** (Right/Up = one delta, Left/Down = another). When the position
leaves the play band the chain parks on the final screen (game over). Emulator proof:
145 passes, deterministic, `PROTO: GAMEOVER-PARKED passes=145 parked=true` (`real03-complete.md`).

## Step 0 — clean reset
`SHIFT` `9` `3` `=` (Reset All → Yes), then `AC`.

## Step 1 — basic overflow by hand (wiki Σ method, already reproduced on this unit)
1. `X=Σ(X,1,1×10⁹` : `ALPHA` `]` `ALPHA` `CALC` `SHIFT` `logab`(Σ) `ALPHA` `]` `SHIFT` `)`(,) `1` `SHIFT` `)` `1` `×10ˣ` `9`, then `EXE` (`=`).
2. Re-enter the short formula `X=Σ(X,1,1` (delete the `×10⁹` tail with `DEL`×3 or retype).
3. `CALC` then `EXE` → **Syntax ERROR** → press `▶` (right).
4. Probe: press `1` — it must **not** appear → cursor is decoupled (basic overflow).
   In this state, typing past 100 chars lands in the cache region → `strlen≥100` is reachable by hand.
   Details + screen tells: `fx991espplus2nd-rop/docs/hardware-procedure.md` §1.

## Step 2 — type the 100-byte payload
Follow the key-by-key table in **`research/CasioEmuX/real03_hand_runbook.md`**
(generated by `keygen.py`, payload md5 `a658df02158fd362305b1cc2a559a806`, 100 bytes, zero-free).
- **354 presses total** = 41-key entry recipe + `AC` `◀` `DEL` + 308 payload presses + trigger.
- Direct-chord bytes: press the listed key (+`SHIFT`/`ALPHA` if listed).
- Walk bytes ("hack-#7 walk", 145 of them): type the listed **seed key**, **wait 2–3 full
  cursor-flash cycles (~1.5–3 s), do nothing**, then press `◀`/`▶` the listed number of
  times — each press advances the symbol by one table entry (HW-verified on this unit).
  If a press was eaten, press the seed again and re-wait. Details: `hardware-procedure.md` §2 (Sheets A/B).

## Step 3 — trigger
`AC` `◀` `EXE`. The strcpy flood fires (ROM 0x6B62, unbounded, dst=src+0x100) and the ROP
chain starts from the tiled stack. **Expected screen: freeze / chaotic glyphs** — that means
it worked. (`ON` resets cleanly; nothing permanent — 26+ emulator runs, mask ROM unmodifiable.)

## Step 4 — play
Just **hold one arrow** (`▶`/`▲` or `◀`/`▼`). The chain polls the keyboard itself every
pass; changing the held arrow steers the cell on the next pass. No key held = drift.
Game over → the chain parks on the final screen. `ON` to reset.
